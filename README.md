# Heroes-IV---Quick-Finish-Retry-Combat-1.0
Two independent mods for Heroes IV.

Quick Finish:
Press Q during a manual battle to immediately continue it using highly accelerated Auto Combat.
The battle continues from its current state. If Quick Finish does not finish the battle within 10 seconds, it switches to normal Auto Combat.

IMPORTANT! Set Resolution Mod to Single core: ON and COLD CPU: OFF.

Retry Combat:
Press X or Esc on the battle results screen to reject the result and retry the battle.
After a manual battle, the fight starts again from the beginning.
After Quick Combat, the simulated result is rejected and the same battle starts in manual mode.

Requirements:
- Heroes IV Complete 3.0 (tested on GOG version)
- Heroes IV Resolution Mod
Tested with:

Resolution Mod 1.51
https://heroes3wog.net/heroes-4-hd-mod-download/

Resolution Mod 1.02
http://h4.heroes.net.pl/uploaded/Download/H4ResMod_1.02.exe

Other versions may also work, but have not been tested.

Installation:

Copy the selected .mod files to:

Heroes IV\mods



For Retry Combat also copy:

Data\layers.dialog.Combat_results.h4d

to:

Heroes IV\Data



Both mods can be used separately or together.
